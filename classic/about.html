My Story

Design is how a space makes you feel.

I believe the best interiors aren’t loud — they’re felt. They hold you the moment you walk in. That belief shapes every project I take on at B Interiors.

With years of experience designing homes and commercial spaces, I work closely with each client to understand how they live, what they love, and what they need — then translate that into a space that is refined, functional and unmistakably theirs.

I stay hands-on from first concept to final styling, so the vision you approve is the one that gets delivered.

What guides every project

Three principles

Timeless over trendy

Classic proportions, honest materials and a restrained palette — spaces that stay beautiful for decades, not seasons.

Personal by design

Every brief begins with listening. Your routines, your taste and your story shape the result — never a formula.

Detail, always

From joinery lines to the way light falls at 5pm, the small decisions are what elevate a room from nice to remarkable.
